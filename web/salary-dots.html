Engineering salaries by university

Each dot is one university, placed by the salary of its graduates in $5,000 bins. Hover or tap a dot to see the university.

All disciplines share the same bins and axes, so their charts can be compared directly. Within each column, the lowest salary sits at the bottom.

Earnings represent the median income reported on IRS tax records for students who graduated from a particular program in 2017–18 or 2018–19. A program is listed if there were more than 15 graduates who had received federal aid.
